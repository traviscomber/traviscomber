## Portfolio rules

- Canonical data before AI.
- Missing information is not zero.
- Human authority remains explicit for consequential decisions.
- Evidence and provenance remain visible.
- Authorization belongs on the server and database boundaries.
- Automation must be observable and recoverable.
- Releases require evidence, gates and rollback readiness.
- Upstream forks and mirrors are research inputs, never presented as original N3uralia authorship.
